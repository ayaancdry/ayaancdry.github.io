Hello! I'm **Ayaan Choudhury**, a final year Mechanical Engineering student with an Interdisciplinary Specialisation in Robotics and Mobility Systems at the [Indian Institute of Technology Jodhpur](https://www.iitj.ac.in/). I conduct research at the intersection of AI/ML, robotics, and 3D vision, with a focus on LiDAR-based localization, SLAM, volumetric mapping, and robot perception.

**Currently:**

- Undergraduate Researcher at the [3DVisLab](https://3dcomputervision.github.io/) (supervised by Prof. Avinash Sharma) — developing a LiDAR localization framework for autonomous navigation in sparse outdoor environments.
- Intern at [BotLab Dynamics](https://www.botlabdynamics.com/) — building real-time volumetric occupancy and signed-distance-field mapping for UAV navigation in unmapped indoor environments.
